Inside the app

A complete virtual subwoofer lab

Sonivex Studio is a native Windows application — no browser, no cloud. Everything computes locally on your machine.

15-part model import

GLB · GLTF · OBJ · FBX. Every mesh stays an individual part — nothing merged, nothing deleted. Part #3 is auto-assigned as the cone; all roles are editable.

Import

Real part customization

Change cone diameter, surround width, magnet stack, box volume, port geometry — the 3D assembly regenerates live and every physics number recomputes instantly.

Design

Play your own MP3s

MP3 · WAV · FLAC · OGG with full transport, waveform and live spectrum. Four synthesized test signals are built in too — no assets needed.

Audio

Physics, not fake wiggles

Your Thiele/Small parameters + enclosure are solved as a lumped electro-mechanical model. The track is filtered through the excursion transfer function — a signed displacement waveform.

Simulation

Xmax & chuffing gauges

Live excursion gauge with Xmax limit, cone velocity and acceleration, SPL estimate, port air velocity against the 17 m/s chuffing threshold. Bottoming-out risk per track.
